Bring (Or Make) Your Own Game Night: Ancient Game Edition

Hosted by Woodbine

11/19/2024 6:30pm

585 Woodward Ave, Ridgewood, NY 11385

From elite strategic pastimes to gamblers' delights, games are as old as civilization itself. Woodbine invites you to take a trip into the distant past to explore the mechanics of chance, competition, and fun from bygone eras. Come play with homemade and 3D printed boards and pieces and experience games ranging from the Royal Game of Ur to Mancala. The twist? Some of these games only survive physically intact with partial-to-no rules. In the spirit of Woodbine's ethos as a cooperative space for practicing autonomy, we invite you to collaborate with your fellow players to fill in the gaps and explore the interactions that result. Or you can start from scratch and make/decorate your own game with materials provided by Woodbine. If all that sounds too daunting, feel free to bring your own easy-to-teach board/card games or select one from Woodbine's library. Regardless of the path you choose, come kick off Game Design Days by meeting new people, playing some (very) old games, and contemplating how previous cultures spent their leisure time - and why.

Join us for a very special Women in Games NYC event. We're part of the Game Design Days circuit - a week of local games industry events leading up to No Quarter {link}.

No Quarter

Hosted by The NYU Game Center

11/23/2024 7:00pm

370 Jay St, Brooklyn, NY 11201

No Quarter is back! On Saturday 23 November No Quarter will be returning for its 2024 edition!
No Quarter is an annual playable exhibition where four newly commissioned works from leading artists working across games are debuted. No Quarter returns to New York this Fall on Saturday the 23rd of November. The event is free to attend, but spaces are limited. Tickets are available at the RSVP link below.
Every year No Quarter commissions new work from leading artists working in games and related fields. These works premiere for one night only as part of the iconic No Quarter exhibition. (see our poster in action below)
The No Quarter 2024 exhibition will take place at 370 Jay Street in Brooklyn on Saturday November 23rd and will be open from 7pm to 10pm.
No Quarter 2024 is curated by Marie Foulston with artwork and visual identity by Jake Terrell.
